This table mirrors the notation in the Phase 1 framework paper. Every symbol
listed here appears in the model specification, the evaluation metrics, or
the pre-registration constants. The canonical value column shows the
pre-registered value where the symbol has one; "—" denotes a variable or a
value estimated from data.

Symbols are grouped thematically: model parameters first, then market and
evaluation quantities, then meta and infrastructure notation.

<BlockMath math="\log \lambda_h = \mu + \alpha_h - \beta_a + \delta_\text{HFA}" />

## Dixon-Coles correction

| Symbol | Definition | Canonical value | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| <InlineMath math="\rho" /> | Dixon-Coles low-score correlation parameter | — | Small negative value, <InlineMath math="\rho \in (-1/(\lambda_h \lambda_a), 0)" /> |
| <InlineMath math="\tau(x,y)" /> | Correction factor for score <InlineMath math="(x,y)" /> | — | Equals 1 for <InlineMath math="\max(x,y) \geq 2" /> |

The correction factor in full:

<BlockMath math="\tau(x,y,\lambda_h,\lambda_a,\rho) = \begin{cases} 1 - \lambda_h\lambda_a\rho & x=0,\; y=0 \\ 1 + \lambda_h\rho & x=0,\; y=1 \\ 1 + \lambda_a\rho & x=1,\; y=0 \\ 1 - \rho & x=1,\; y=1 \\ 1 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}" />

## Market and edge quantities

| Symbol | Definition | Canonical value | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| <InlineMath math="p_m" /> | Model-implied probability for outcome | — | <InlineMath math="p_m \in [0,1]" /> |
| <InlineMath math="q_\text{raw}" /> | Raw bookmaker decimal odds (inverted) | — | Before margin removal |
| <InlineMath math="q_d" /> | De-vigged market probability | — | Power-method de-vigging; <InlineMath math="\sum_j q_{d,j} = 1" /> |
| <InlineMath math="E" /> | Edge | — | <InlineMath math="E = p_m - q_d" />; positive = model higher than market |
| <InlineMath math="\varepsilon" /> | Edge threshold | 0.03 (mainline) / 0.05 (longshot) | Pre-registered; governs gate status |
| <InlineMath math="k" /> | Power-method exponent for de-vigging | — | Estimated to zero overround |

The power-method de-vigging formula:

<BlockMath math="q_{d,i} = \frac{o_i^{-1/k}}{\displaystyle\sum_j o_j^{-1/k}}" />

where <InlineMath math="o_i" /> is the decimal odds for outcome <InlineMath math="i" />.

## Infrastructure and audit notation

| Symbol | Definition | Example |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| `snapshot_id` | ISO-8601 timestamp identifying a data bundle | `2026-06-11T04:00Z` |

| `model_id` | Model identifier | `M0`, `M1`, `M2`, `M3`, `M_STAR` |
